<h1>Maven Market</h1>

 

<h2>Description</h2>
Adventure Works – Sales Analysis Dashboard

Project Overview

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard using the Adventure Works dataset to analyze sales performance, product trends, customer behavior, and regional sales. The project transforms raw business data into meaningful insights to support data-driven decision-making.

Key Features

Analyzed total revenue, profit, orders, and sales trends.
Evaluated product performance and identified top-selling products.
Explored customer purchasing behavior and sales distribution.
Compared sales performance across different regions.
Created interactive visuals, KPIs, and DAX measures for business analysis.


Business Value: Provided a clear overview of sales performance and helped identify growth opportunities, high-performing products, and areas for improvement.
